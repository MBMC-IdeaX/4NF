## Decisions on file
- Tenant = transport company/committee; individual bus owners are members (role `bus_owner`) who see only their buses.
- Company levy = flat amount per bus per day it ran.
- Agreements: paper + scan AND in-app acceptance; both kept.
- Onboarding is done by Bhada staff (admin side). Owners do not self-register companies or buses.
- Routes are built by reviewers from the route permit, by hand (no AI for now).
- Money: Bhada never holds funds (partner-held). Revenue = per-active-bus monthly fee (or growth share), flat top-up fee, flat payout fee. No fare commission.
- Pilot with 1–2 companies first; the transport authority later.
- Design: familiar + minimal (white, one red #c8102e, Mukta), Khand only on plates.
- Research: docs/legal/2026-10-03-onboarding-research.md
